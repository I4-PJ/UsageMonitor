Usage Monitor - macOS

Requires macOS 15 or newer.

1. Choose the osx-arm64 ZIP for Apple Silicon, or osx-x64 for an Intel Mac.
2. Extract the ZIP and drag UsageMonitor.app into Applications.
3. Install or open Codex and sign in with ChatGPT as the same macOS user.
   Official setup: https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/codex/cli
4. Open UsageMonitor.app. Use its menu-bar icon to show details or quit.

The .NET runtime is included. Codex itself is installed separately.
This prerelease has no Developer ID signature and has not been notarized.
macOS may require approval for this app in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
Settings and history are saved under ~/Library/Application Support/UsageMonitor.
Quit the app before replacing an installed bundle during an update.

Source, help, and releases: https://github.com/I4-PJ/UsageMonitor
Licenses are inside UsageMonitor.app/Contents/MacOS, including ThirdPartyLicenses.
